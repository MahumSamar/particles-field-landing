Particle Field

One THREE.Points cloud, a procedural sprite, and a CPU solver. No bloom pass, no depth of field, no texture, no model.

Scroll

It arrives

The field dollies in from 106 world units to 16, interpolated in log space so the approach reads as even rather than rushing at the end. Every particle starts as identical fine dust and grows into its own size class on the way in.

It answers the cursor

Move across it. Particles inside the radius are not pushed away with a falloff — they are relocated to sit exactly on the rim of a disc, measured perpendicular to the view axis so the hole stays circular however far the field has rotated. It snaps in four times faster than it relaxes out.

Any shape

The silhouette is a swappable sampler. The superellipse is one implementation; an SVG path is another. Try the shape control, top right.

Then it goes

Each particle travels along a fixed random direction while growing and softening, so the cloud evaporates instead of merely flying apart.
